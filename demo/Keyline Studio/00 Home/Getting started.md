# Welcome to Keyline

This fictional design studio is a place to explore Keyline’s light and dark surfaces, typography, and connected notes. Everything is editable. No real user information is included.

## Three places to begin

1. Open [[Keyline Studio]] to see the work on the desk.
2. Explore [[Field Notes]] to follow links between projects, research, and journals.
3. Open Settings → Keyline to adjust typography, icons, and editing behaviour.

## Try it while editing

- Select text to reveal the formatting toolbar.
- Type / on an empty line to open the insert menu.
- Type @ to choose a date or page. Without a selection, it remains an ordinary @ character, so email addresses are safe.
- Drag the handle beside a paragraph to move the entire block.

> [!note] English and Chinese interfaces
> The companion plugins follow Obsidian’s interface language. Restart after changing the language. Existing note contents, saved paths, and user-defined names are never translated.

The theme works on its own. Keyline interactions and Bucket Sidebar are optional companions. Marginalia is not included in this example.
