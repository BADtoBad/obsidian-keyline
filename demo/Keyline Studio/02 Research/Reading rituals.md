# Reading rituals

In our fictional study, participants described three different reasons to mark a passage: to remember it, to question it, or to share it. These intentions should not be forced into the same workflow.

## Before reading

A short question provides a reason to pay attention. The question may change; keeping its history is more useful than pretending it was fixed from the start.

## During reading

Capture should be quick. A note can begin as a few words, with the source nearby and no requirement to choose a folder immediately.

## After reading

Ask which passage changed the reader’s thinking. Returning to everything is not a realistic goal; choosing one connection is.

> [!warning] Demonstration data
> These research observations are invented for this example Vault. They are not findings from an actual study.
