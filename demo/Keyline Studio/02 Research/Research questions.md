# Research questions

## Reader experience

- When does a passage become meaningful enough to keep?
- What helps someone understand their own note one month later?
- Is an annotation more useful beside a passage or in a separate review?

## Interface behaviour

- Can controls appear on demand without becoming difficult to discover?
- Does a consistent heading colour make scanning more reliable?
- What breaks first when the sidebar becomes narrow?

## Method

Keep usability, comprehension, and preference separate. A person can prefer an interface that performs worse on a particular task; neither observation should erase the other.
