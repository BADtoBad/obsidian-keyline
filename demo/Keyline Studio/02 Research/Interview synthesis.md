# Interview synthesis

This fictional synthesis shows how a small team might turn observations into useful next questions.

| Observation | Interpretation | Next question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Readers kept a book open while writing | Source context may reduce hesitation | Can the reference stay beside the note? |
| Several highlights had no accompanying thought | Saving and understanding are different | What makes a return visit useful? |
| A long tag list was often skipped | Classification may be premature | Can links emerge after capture? |

## Do not conclude yet

We have not established whether faster annotation leads to better recall. A usability result answers whether an interaction works, not whether the product improves learning.
