# Sunday Letter

A short dispatch about making useful things. One observation, one experiment, one question to take into the week.

## Issue 04 — Room to think

**Opening:** A workspace does not have to display everything it knows. The most useful detail is often the one that appears at the right moment.

**Experiment:** Remove one persistent control from a prototype. Give it a clear keyboard shortcut and a contextual home. Observe what people actually miss.

**Question:** Which part of your workspace is asking for attention without earning it?
