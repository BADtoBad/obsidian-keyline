# Field Notes

A reading companion for people who like to think in the margins.

**Stage** · Prototype

## The idea

Collect a passage, add a thought, and return to it in context. The book remains the centre of attention.

> [!note] North star
> Help readers build a relationship with an idea — not a collection of highlights.
