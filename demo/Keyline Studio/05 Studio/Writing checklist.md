# Writing checklist

## Before sharing

- [ ] Does the first paragraph explain why this matters?
- [ ] Is each claim supported or clearly labelled as a hypothesis?
- [ ] Can the reader find the next action without searching?
- [ ] Have repeated ideas been combined?
- [ ] Are links descriptive and useful?
- [ ] Is any private information unnecessary to the purpose?

## Read aloud

Long sentences often hide two separate ideas. Split them when the relationship becomes clearer, not merely to meet a word count.

## Last pass

Check names, dates, and the destination of every link. A polished sentence cannot compensate for a broken reference.
