# Decision log

## D-003 · Keep capture lightweight

**Decision:** Do not require a tag before saving an annotation.  
**Reason:** Classification interrupted the reading loop in our fictional study.  
**Revisit when:** Review becomes difficult without more structure.

## D-002 · Keep one heading accent

**Decision:** Use a consistent blue for all heading levels.  
**Reason:** Typography already communicates hierarchy; multiple hues added noise.  
**Revisit when:** Accessibility testing identifies a specific contrast issue.

## D-001 · Keep original notes portable

**Decision:** Store ordinary Markdown and links.  
**Reason:** The workspace should remain useful without its visual enhancements.
