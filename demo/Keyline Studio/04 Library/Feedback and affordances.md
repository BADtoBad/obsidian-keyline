# Feedback and affordances

Reading prompt: *The Design of Everyday Things*, Don Norman.

## My question

How does an interface help a person understand what just happened?

## Working note

An action needs a perceivable consequence. That consequence does not always require a toast or a modal. A changed state, a moved object, or a visible result can explain the action more directly.

## Apply it

After saving a workspace, show its name in the switcher. After adding a link, let the linked text be the confirmation. Reserve interruptions for cases that need a decision.
