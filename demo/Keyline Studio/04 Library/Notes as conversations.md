# Notes as conversations

Reading prompt: *How to Take Smart Notes*, Sönke Ahrens.

## Working note

A useful note does more than preserve a source. It says what the writer thinks, what the idea relates to, and why it might matter later.

## A small experiment

Take one highlighted passage and write three sentences: the claim in your own words, a connection to an existing question, and one reason to be cautious.

## Connection

The [[Field Notes]] review view should help a reader continue a thought, rather than merely display a list of everything they saved.
